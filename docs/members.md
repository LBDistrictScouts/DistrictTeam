## Start with the information you have

Upload a UTF-8 CSV and map its units, teams and roles to records already in District Team. Review each distinct source combination on one screen before choosing what to import.

You can import member and contact details while leaving an appointment unmapped, or skip an appointment while keeping the member information. The import does not create teams or roles for you.

## Make a deliberate import

Choose which units to include, check the destination for each mapping and import the selected records together. Invalid rows are reported so you can correct the source or mapping and try again.

Existing members are matched by membership number. Contacts and appointments are matched to their member and destination, which helps repeated imports update matching records instead of duplicating them.

## Reuse your mappings

Explicit mapping choices can be remembered between uploads, giving your team a quicker start next time. Blank choices leave saved defaults in place, and the mapping screen remains available for review and edits.
