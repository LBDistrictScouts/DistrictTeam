## One view of the whole team

Build a connected view of teams from district leadership through groups and sections. Explore the wider structure or focus on the team you are working with.

Each team is linked to its group and, where relevant, its section. Roles belong to teams, and appointments connect those roles to members. That gives volunteers a shared picture of who is involved and how each team relates to the next.

## Keep the structure in order

Arrange teams into the right hierarchy and set their order to match how your district works. Move through a team’s branch while keeping the rest of the structure in place.

Create the teams and roles your district needs, and use a standard group template to establish a consistent starting point across groups.

## Make changes with context

Team details, role assignments and appointments stay connected as the structure grows. A team can be associated with a group and an optional section, giving each part of the district a clear place in the wider picture.
