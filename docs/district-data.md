## Share a consistent source of information

District Team can import group and section details from DistrictCoreData, including names, ordering, section types and meeting details. Shared identifiers keep those records connected across systems.

Updates refresh the imported details while retaining records that are absent from a later feed. The sync validates its downloads before saving changes.

## Connect teams to their place

Every team belongs to a group. A section team can also be linked to the section it supports, while district and group leadership teams can be associated with their respective group without a section.

When a section moves between groups in the source data, its linked teams follow the updated group while the team hierarchy and appointments remain in place.
